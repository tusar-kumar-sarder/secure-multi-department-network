Notes
-----
- VLAN 10 gateway: 192.168.10.1/24 (HR)
- VLAN 20 gateway: 192.168.20.1/24 (IT)
- VLAN 30 gateway: 192.168.30.1/24 (ACCOUNTS)
- VLAN 99 gateway: 192.168.99.1/24
- WAN G0/0: 203.0.113.2/30; default next hop: 203.0.113.1
- G0/2: 10.0.0.1/30
- OSPF process 1, router ID 1.1.1.1
- NAT/PAT uses ACL 1 and G0/0 overload
- IT-BLOCK-ACCOUNTS denies IT subnet traffic to ACCOUNTS subnet
- Verify interfaces, trunking, OSPF neighbors, NAT, DHCP, and ACL behavior in Packet Tracer.
